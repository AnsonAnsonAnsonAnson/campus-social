# App Store listing

Paste into App Store Connect → your app → **App Information** / the version page.
Set **Primary Language** to **Chinese (Traditional)** (App Information), then add
**English (U.K.)** or **English (U.S.)** as a second localization and paste the
English text there. Keep "CUHK" in descriptive text only (see README, guideline 5.2).

Character limits: name 30, subtitle 30, promotional text 170, keywords 100
(comma separated, no spaces needed), description 4000.

**Name:** Campus Social

**Subtitle:** 中大學會活動・一按報名

**Promotional text:**
追蹤你喜歡的學會，第一時間知道新活動。一按報名、二維碼門票入場，額滿亦可候補，有名額自動補上。

**Description:**
Campus Social 是為香港中文大學學生而設的校園活動 app。

追蹤學會
・瀏覽所有已登記的學會，追蹤你感興趣的
・在「探索」優先看到你追蹤的學會的活動
・學會發佈新活動時即時通知你

報名活動
・一按報名，二維碼門票即時存入「門票」
・額滿可加入候補名單，有人取消時自動為你補上並通知你
・活動前一小時提醒你，活動改時間、地點或取消都會通知你
・一鍵加入手機日曆

校園日曆
・大學及你所屬書院的重要日子，連同已報名的活動，一個日曆睇晒

學會幹事專用
・建立及編輯活動、設定名額、候補及報名時段
・用手機相機掃描門票簽到，或輸入 8 位代碼
・匯出參加者名單（CSV），向報名者提問
・管理幹事會成員，申請「探索」頁宣傳橫幅

只限持有中大電郵（@link.cuhk.edu.hk 或 @cuhk.edu.hk）的學生及教職員登入，無需密碼。
本 app 由學生開發，並非香港中文大學官方 app。

## English

**Name:** Campus Social

**Subtitle:** CUHK society events & tickets

**Promotional text:**
Follow the societies you love and never miss an event. Sign up in one tap, get a QR ticket, and join the waitlist when it's full: you're moved up automatically.

**Description:**
Campus Social is the events app for CUHK students.

FOLLOW SOCIETIES
• Browse every registered society and follow the ones you like
• See events from societies you follow first on Discover
• Get notified when they post something new

SIGN UP FOR EVENTS
• Register in one tap and your QR ticket is saved in Tickets
• Join the waitlist when an event is full; you're moved up and notified automatically if someone cancels
• Reminders an hour before, and a heads-up if an event moves or is cancelled
• Add any event to your phone's calendar

CAMPUS CALENDAR
• University and college dates alongside the events you're going to

FOR SOCIETY COMMITTEES
• Create events with capacity, waitlists and sign-up windows
• Check people in by scanning tickets or typing an 8-character code
• Export the attendee list as CSV and ask attendees a question when they sign up
• Manage your committee and request promotional banners on Discover

Sign in with your CUHK email (@link.cuhk.edu.hk or @cuhk.edu.hk). No password needed.
Campus Social is built by students and is not an official app of The Chinese University of Hong Kong.

**Keywords:**
CUHK,university,society,club,events,tickets,campus,student,college,orientation,Hong Kong,signup

**What's New (1.0):**
First release: follow societies, sign up for events, QR tickets, waitlists, reminders and a campus calendar.

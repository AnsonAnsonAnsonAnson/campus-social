## English

**Name:** Campus Social

**Subtitle:** CUHK society events & tickets

**Promotional text:**
Follow the societies you love and never miss an event. Sign up in one tap, get a QR ticket, and join the waitlist when it's full: you're moved up automatically.

**Description:**
Campus Social is the events app for CUHK students.

FOLLOW SOCIETIES
• Browse and search every registered society and follow the ones you like
• See events from societies you follow first on Discover
• Get notified when they post something new

SIGN UP FOR EVENTS
• Register in one tap and your QR ticket is saved in Tickets, even offline
• Tap the heart to save an event for later
• Join the waitlist when an event is full; you're moved up and notified automatically if someone cancels
• Reminders an hour before, and a heads-up if an event moves or is cancelled
• Pay for paid events straight to the society by FPS, PayMe or cash

MEMBERSHIPS AND MERCH
• Join a society in one tap and keep your member card on your phone
• Order society hoodies and T-shirts and show a QR code at pickup

CAMPUS CALENDAR
• University and college dates alongside the events you're going to

FOR SOCIETY COMMITTEES
• Create events with capacity, waitlists, sign-up windows and ticket prices
• Scan tickets, member cards and pickup codes with your phone's camera
• Member lists with Excel import and export, merch orders and payment matching
• Manage your committee and request promotional banners on Discover

Sign in with your CUHK email (@link.cuhk.edu.hk or @cuhk.edu.hk). No password needed.
Campus Social is built by students and is not an official app of The Chinese University of Hong Kong.

**Keywords:**
CUHK,university,society,club,events,tickets,campus,student,college,orientation,Hong Kong,signup

**What's New (1.0):**
First release: follow societies, save and sign up for events, QR tickets, waitlists, member cards, society merch and a campus calendar.

## App Review information

**Sign-in required:** yes. Give the review account (see README › Launching, step 7).

**Notes** (paste into App Review › Notes):

> Campus Social is for students and staff of The Chinese University of Hong Kong. Accounts are limited to CUHK email addresses, which receive a one-time code. For review, please tap Profile › Sign in › "Sign in with a password instead" and use the account above. It is also a committee member of a test society, so you can try the organiser tools (Profile › the society › New event, Scan tickets).
>
> Browsing Discover, Calendar and Societies works without an account.
>
> Payments: paid events, society membership fees and society merch (T-shirts, hoodies) are paid by the student directly to the student society by FPS, PayMe or cash, for real-world events and physical goods. The app never processes money (guideline 3.1.3(e)).
>
> User-generated content: new societies and banners are approved by administrators before they appear. Any event or society can be reported (⋯ › Report) or hidden, reports are handled within 24 hours, and the terms (linked on the sign-in screen) have a zero-tolerance rule.
>
> Campus Social is built by students and is not an official app of the university.

## App Privacy (App Store Connect › App Privacy)

Tracking: **No**. Every type below is **linked to the user** and **not used for tracking**.

| Data type | Purpose |
|---|---|
| Contact info › Email address | App functionality |
| Contact info › Name | App functionality |
| Contact info › Phone number (only when joining a society) | App functionality |
| User content › Photos (profile, society uploads, optional payment screenshots) | App functionality |
| User content › Other user content (sign-up answers, reports) | App functionality |
| Identifiers › User ID | App functionality |
| Purchases › Purchase history (orders and tickets with societies) | App functionality |
| Other data › Student ID (only when joining a society) | App functionality |

Not collected: location, contacts, browsing history, health, financial info, diagnostics, advertising data.

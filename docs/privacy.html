私隱政策

最後更新：2026年10月27日

Campus Social（「本 app」）由中大學生獨立開發及營運，並非香港中文大學官方 app。本政策說明我們收集甚麼資料、點樣使用，以及你可以點樣刪除。我們遵守香港《個人資料（私隱）條例》。

付款

付款經轉數快、PayMe 或現金直接付給學會，本 app 不會經手任何款項，亦不會收集你的銀行或信用卡資料。付款截圖上傳前會在手機上縮細，並會自動刪除：付款確認後 30 日、取消後 7 日，最長保留 90 日。

選擇網上付款（信用卡、Apple Pay、Google Pay、支付寶、微信支付）時，你會在 Stripe 的付款頁面輸入付款資料，本 app 看不到亦不會儲存你的卡號。我們會把你的電郵、訂單編號、貨品及金額交給 Stripe 處理付款，Stripe 會按其私隱政策處理這些資料。學會設定網上收款時，負責人的身份證明及銀行資料由 Stripe 直接收集及核實，本 app 只會知道核實結果。

服務供應商

  • Supabase：儲存資料庫及上傳的圖片。
  • Expo 及 Apple 推送通知服務：傳送通知到你的裝置。
  • 電郵服務供應商：寄出一次性登入驗證碼。

以上供應商只會按我們的指示處理資料，資料可能儲存在香港以外的地方。

保留及刪除

你可以隨時在「個人 › 刪除帳戶」刪除帳戶。你的個人檔案、追蹤、收藏、門票、通知及推送代碼會即時刪除；你建立的學會活動會留給學會。如需索取或更正你的資料，請電郵至 REPLACE_WITH_SUPPORT_EMAIL，我們會在 30 日內回覆。

兒童

本 app 只供持有中大電郵的大學學生及教職員使用，並非為 13 歲以下兒童而設。

Privacy Policy

Last updated: 27 October 2026

Campus Social ("the app") is built and run independently by CUHK students. It is not an official app of The Chinese University of Hong Kong. This policy explains what we collect, why, and how you can delete it. We follow Hong Kong's Personal Data (Privacy) Ordinance.

We do not collect your location, contacts or other photos on your device. There are no ads and no third-party analytics or tracking, and we never sell or rent your data.

Payments

You pay societies directly by FPS, PayMe or cash. The app never handles money and never collects bank or card details. Payment screenshots are shrunk on your phone before upload and deleted automatically: 30 days after the payment is confirmed, 7 days after a cancellation, and after 90 days at most.

If you pay online (card, Apple Pay, Google Pay, Alipay or WeChat Pay), you enter your payment details on Stripe's payment page; the app never sees or stores your card number. We give Stripe your email, the order reference, the items and the amount so it can process the payment, and Stripe handles them under its privacy policy. When a society sets up online payments, Stripe collects and checks the committee member's ID and bank details directly; the app only learns whether the check passed.

Service providers

  • Supabase stores the database and uploaded images.
  • Expo and Apple Push Notification service deliver notifications to your device.
  • An email provider sends one-time sign-in codes.

These providers process data only on our instructions. Data may be stored outside Hong Kong.

Keeping and deleting your data

You can delete your account at any time in Profile › Delete account. Your profile, follows, saved events, tickets, notifications and push tokens are deleted straight away; events you created stay with the society. To access or correct your data, email REPLACE_WITH_SUPPORT_EMAIL and we will reply within 30 days.

Children

The app is only for university students and staff with a CUHK email address. It is not meant for children under 13.

Changes

If this policy changes in a meaningful way, we will tell you in the app.
